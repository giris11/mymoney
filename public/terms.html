What this is

MyMoney is a free, personal, open-source finance app that runs in your browser. It is a personal project, provided as-is, with no warranty and no guarantee of availability, correctness or fitness for any particular purpose.

Your data is your responsibility

All of your data is stored in your own browser, and optionally in a folder of its own in your own Dropbox if you turn sync on. Nobody else holds a copy. That means:

  • Clearing your browser's site data, or a browser evicting storage to reclaim space, will delete the app's data on that device.
  • Keeping backups is up to you. The app provides a one-tap backup export, and will remind you if you have not taken one for a while.
  • No one can recover your data for you, because no one else has it.

Not financial advice

MyMoney records and summarises figures you enter or import. It does not provide financial, investment, tax or accounting advice, and its reports should not be relied upon as a substitute for professional advice or for your bank's own records. Always check important figures against your bank statements.

Exchange rates

Where currency conversion is shown, rates come from free public sources and are daily reference rates, not live market prices. They are indicative only and should not be used for trading or for any purpose requiring accurate real-time pricing.
